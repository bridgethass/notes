# Agentic Coding

**Summary**: Using AI coding agents (such as Claude Code) to write, run, and maintain code, plus views on how AI changes technical work.
**Last updated**: 2026-11-19

---

- [How to be more ambitious: A tutorial for humans with models (Felix Rieseberg)](https://felixrieseberg.com/how-to-be-more-ambitious-a-tutorial-for-humans-with-models/): Argues that people underuse AI mainly because they doubt what's possible. Covers four kinds of ambition with models: Speed, Altitude (ask for outcomes, not tasks), Depth (expert-level methods, outside verification), and Range (borrow from other fields). *Keywords: AI ambition, prompting, self-efficacy, outcomes, verification*
- [Reflections on AI as an Educator (Spatial Thoughts)](https://spatialthoughts.com/2026/05/12/reflections-on-ai/): Argues that AI coding tools make domain expertise and judgment more valuable. Routine coding can be handed off, while people focus on picking problems, choosing data, and validating results. *Keywords: AI, education, domain expertise, judgment, AI-assisted coding*
- [Using AI Without Losing Your Soul – Drew Breunig (CNG 2026 Keynote)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qqSw23XQTxc): Drew Breunig's keynote at the CNG 2026 conference on using AI thoughtfully. The video description couldn't be retrieved, so this summary is based on the title only. See [[Cloud_Native]] for the Cloud-Native Geospatial Forum. *Keywords: AI, keynote, CNG 2026, Drew Breunig, responsible AI*
