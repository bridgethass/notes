# Remote Sensing

**Summary**: Collecting and analyzing data about the Earth from satellites and aircraft, including training resources and analysis tools.
**Last updated**: 2026-11-18

---

- [Daily Fire Dashboard (Spatial Thoughts)](https://spatialthoughts.github.io/firms-dashboard/): A dashboard that maps active fire detections from the past 24 hours, using NASA FIRMS near-real-time MODIS and VIIRS satellite data. See [[Geospatial]]. *Keywords: wildfire, NASA FIRMS, MODIS, VIIRS, dashboard, near-real-time*
- [NASA ARSET – Applied Remote Sensing Training Program](https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/data/projects/arset): NASA's free training on applying satellite data to air quality, agriculture, disasters, water, conservation ([[Ecology]]), wildfires, and climate resilience. Courses range from beginner fundamentals to advanced topics like NISAR radar. *Keywords: NASA, satellite data, training, free courses, Earth observation*
