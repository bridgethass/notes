# Training

**Summary**: Courses, workshops, and training programs for learning geospatial, remote sensing, and AI-assisted coding skills.
**Last updated**: 2026-11-18

---

- [NASA ARSET – Applied Remote Sensing Training Program](https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/data/projects/arset): NASA's free training on applying satellite data ([[Remote_Sensing]]) to air quality, agriculture, disasters, water, conservation ([[Ecology]]), wildfires, and climate resilience. Courses range from beginner fundamentals to advanced topics like NISAR radar. *Keywords: NASA, satellite data, training, free courses, Earth observation*
- [Agentic Coding for Geospatial (Full Course)](https://courses.spatialthoughts.com/agentic-coding-geospatial): Ujaval Gandhi's course on using Claude Code ([[Agentic_Coding]]) for [[Geospatial]] data science. Covers CLAUDE.md, plan mode, skills, and MCP servers (QGIS, Colab), with projects like hotspot mapping and building detection from aerial imagery with GeoAI ([[Remote_Sensing]]). *Keywords: Claude Code, geospatial, MCP, QGIS, GeoAI, course*
